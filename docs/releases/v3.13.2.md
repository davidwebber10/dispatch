# Dispatch v3.13.2 — the phone app fits the screen again

## The change

On an iPhone, the installed app now fits between the status bar and the bottom of the screen.
Since v3.12.0 the bottom tab bar was pushed partly off-screen.

## What was wrong

v3.12.0 made the status bar solid (to remove the fade iOS drew over the header). With a solid
status bar the page starts below it, but the app was still sized to the full screen height, so
it overflowed at the bottom by exactly the status bar's height. The app is now sized to the
visible area.

## Updating

Run `dispatch update`, then close and reopen the app on your phone.
