# Dispatch v2.47.4 — the CLI status card moves under the terminal

In a CLI thread (Codex or Claude in terminal mode), the latest outcome card and the "This
thread is asking you" strip now sit under the terminal, as they do in a Pretty thread, and a
prompt submit no longer resizes the terminal. (PR #74.)

## The change

- **Under the terminal.** The outcome card (DONE, BLOCKED, NEEDS YOU) and the amber "This
  thread is asking you" strip moved from above the terminal to below it. The Pretty-mode hint
  stays at the top.
- **The card stays during a turn.** While a turn runs, the last outcome stays in place, dimmed
  and marked "Last turn". The new outcome replaces it when the turn ends.
- **Fewer terminal resizes.** The area under the terminal keeps its height while a turn runs
  and for a moment after it ends. The terminal resizes at most once per turn, and not at all
  when you submit.

## What was wrong

The card hid when a turn started and came back when it ended, and the question strip went away
when you answered. Each change resized the terminal. Codex in inline mode answers every resize
by writing about 1000 lines of its history again, so each turn made Codex rewrite its history
two or three times. Since v2.47.3 the view no longer jumps when this happens; this release
removes most of the rewrites.

## Updating

Web app only. Run `dispatch update`, then reload the page.
