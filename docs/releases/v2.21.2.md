# Dispatch v2.21.2 — the phantom "Working…" goes away

## The change

**Reopened threads no longer claim to be working when they aren't.** A Claude,
Codex, or Grok Pretty thread could show the "Working…" shimmer forever after a
reopen or a daemon restart, even though the turn had long finished. The chat
now settles the spinner whenever the daemon itself knows the thread is idle —
and a thread that genuinely IS mid-turn keeps its true spinner.

**A little more air at the bottom of the chat.** The last row — usually that
same Working… indicator — sat 8px off the composer; it now gets the same
spacing as the top of the thread.

## What was wrong

The spinner is latched by streaming events and cleared by a turn's closing
`result`. But a replayed history can legitimately end without one: a restored
Grok session replays whole messages with no per-turn footers, and any thread
whose process died mid-turn never wrote its closing result. Replaying such a
history left the spinner latched with nothing to clear it.

## Updating

Needs a **daemon rebuild/restart** (`dispatch update`) — the settle is emitted
by the daemon; the padding rides the web bundle it ships.
