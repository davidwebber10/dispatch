# v2.25.0 — the update is now a game

The pixel rain you pop with your cursor during a Dispatch update keeps score.

## Update-rain pop game

- A **POPS counter** sits in the top-right of the rain. Every drop you
  explode increments it.
- Your **high score and its date** show under the counter ("BEST 87 · Aug 12")
  — the target for the round. Pass it and the counter goes hot: **NEW BEST!**
- The high score persists in localStorage, written eagerly on every
  record-breaking pop — the update ends in a hard page reload, and a
  save-at-the-end would race it.
- **Beat your best and the restart throws a party**: once Dispatch is back in
  the normal view, fireworks fill the screen behind a glass trophy card with
  your score and the best you beat. One-shot (the flag clears on first read)
  and freshness-gated (a flag older than an hour is dropped silently, so a
  failed update can't celebrate days later).
- Reduced-motion users keep the calm static rain: no counter, no fireworks.

## Updating

**Web-only** — a browser refresh is enough. The game appears during your
next update.
