# Dispatch v2.47.2 — recaps that are never skipped, and two ledger fixes

Three fixes from the first live days of ledger titles. The overseer now posts the recap it
prepares, even when the recap is only one line; a parked decision can take its answer later;
and a title that the overseer gave an agent's decision counts when it sends it to you. (PR #71,
PR #72.)

## The change

- **The recap is never skipped, and it is as long as the news.** When the overseer prepares a
  recap, the daemon now tells it that this step posts nothing and that it must post the recap
  in the same reply. When nothing is new, no agent is running and no work has finished, the
  recap is one line: the count line. When there are new or decided items, the overseer posts
  them. The overseer no longer says that it posted a recap unless the recap is in that reply.
- **A parked decision can take a later answer.** When you park a decision and give the answer
  days later, the overseer records your answer on the parked item. Only your words from after
  the park count. The answer shows in the next recap, and the item leaves the card's Parked
  list. A parked item still cannot be parked again or withdrawn, and a parked "go" item still
  needs its ID or the action word in your answer.
- **A title set before counts.** When the overseer titles a decision that an agent proposed and
  then sends it to you, the daemon uses that title. Before, the send failed until the overseer
  gave the title again. A blank title in the send is still refused.

## What was wrong

An overseer prepared a recap, posted nothing, and said "The recap is posted". The step that
prepares a recap also marks its lines as shown, so decisions that were answered or withdrawn
never reached you in the chat. A decision that you parked could not take its answer later, so
the overseer recorded the answer as a new note and the decision stayed on the card as parked.

## Updating

Daemon only. Run `dispatch update`. Each overseer gets the new recap and ledger rules when it
restarts, which `dispatch update` does.
