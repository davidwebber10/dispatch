# Dispatch v4.5.4 — no more lines on the left of cards

## The change

- **No coloured or grey line on the left edge of a card any more.** This applies to:
  - The Insight box in chat. It keeps its light tint and its INSIGHT label.
  - The thinking text that you open in chat.
  - Quotes in Markdown. They now have a light background.
  - The "Needs you" cards in the Chat projects panel.
  - The quoted message in a Control Plane notice.
- A test now stops these lines from coming back.

## Updating

Web only: run `dispatch update`, then reload the app.
