# Dispatch v2.13.1 — Grok threads show up in the sidebar

## The change

Grok threads appear in the THREADS list, with their own provider dot.

## What was wrong

v2.12.0 added Grok as a provider through core, the types and the New Thread modal, but the
sidebar filtered its thread list against an inline `['claude-code', 'codex', 'shell']`. A
Grok thread therefore spawned, ran, and was dropped from the list — invisible, with no way
to reach it.

Three more inline copies of that same list sat within 500 lines of it, governing row style,
the alerts bell and the context menu. Adding a provider needed four coordinated edits and
got one.

They are now two exported constants, `AGENT_TYPES` and `THREAD_TYPES`, asserted directly in
a test. The next provider is one edit.

## Updating

**Web-only** — a browser refresh is enough.
