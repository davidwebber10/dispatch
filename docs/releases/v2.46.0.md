# Dispatch v2.46.0 — the pinned recap card

The Control Plane screen now shows the project's decision ledger as a live card. You see every
open decision with its full context without reading a recap, the overseer's recaps shrink to
news, and a ledger number never appears without its question. (PR #67.)

## The change

- **A live "Needs you" card.** On desktop it sits at the top of the Details tab in the right
  pane, above "Ongoing work". On mobile it sits at the top of the Work tab. New items and the
  top 5 show as full cards: the question, the context, each option with its effect, the
  recommendation and why, what happens without an answer, what it holds up and the source.
  Every other open decision is one line; a click opens it. Your actions, items on their
  defaults, decided items, untriaged items and parked items fold to a count. The project rules
  are a read-only list.
- **Clicks fill your message box.** A click on an option adds an answer such as `N17: A` to the
  message box; an Approve row adds `N34: approve`; a Done link adds `N55: done`. Several clicks
  build one message. Nothing is sent until you press Enter, so your own words still decide, as
  before.
- **Ledger numbers carry their question.** On the Control Plane screen, every ledger number in
  the chat shows as a chip with its question, in the overseer's replies, your messages, the
  notices and the status cards. A click opens that item on the card. Numbers in code, links
  and paths stay plain text.
- **Recaps are news.** The overseer no longer pastes the ledger into its recap. A recap holds a
  header, one line for each new item, what runs, and what finished, with the decided items.
  The overseer never writes a bare ledger number or a range of them.
- **Fewer interim recaps.** The 20-minute interim recap comes only when a new item waits on you
  since the last recap. A finished agent alone does not trigger it.
- **The card stays current.** The daemon sends a `ledger:changed` event after every ledger
  change, and the card loads again. When the right pane is closed, its button shows the number
  of open decisions.

## What was wrong

Overseers either pasted the whole ledger into each recap (most of a 7,000 to 17,000 character
recap was the same list again) or rewrote it in their own words, which dropped the options and
the defaults and named items by bare numbers such as "N11 and N52 are done". Desktop had no
"needs you" display at all.

## Updating

Daemon and web app. Run `dispatch update`. The card works as soon as the web app loads again.
Each overseer gets the new recap rules when it restarts, which the update does; you do not need
a new session.
