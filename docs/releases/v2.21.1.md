# Dispatch v2.21.1 — Grok keeps its history, and the updater goes glass

## The change

**Reopening a Grok thread shows the whole conversation again.** A Grok turn
streams word by word, so one long reply blew past the chat's fast-open replay
window — a reopened thread showed nothing but the token footer, with no way to
load the rest. Grok threads now replay their full history on open.

**The phantom "Load earlier messages" button is gone on Grok threads.** It
rendered on brand-new threads with nothing earlier to load, and tapping it did
nothing. Grok has no pageable transcript, so the control no longer appears.

**The self-update screen goes liquid glass.** While Dispatch updates itself,
the progress card is now a translucent, blurred glass block — the terminal rain
falls visibly behind it instead of being blacked out.

## What was wrong

Grok's chat reused Claude's history plumbing, but two of its assumptions don't
hold for Grok: the bounded fast-open replay assumes anything it cuts is
recoverable from a transcript on disk (Grok has none to page), and the
"earlier messages" affordance assumes messages carry transcript identities
(Grok's don't). Claude and Codex threads are unaffected.

## Updating

Needs a **daemon rebuild/restart** (`dispatch update`) for the Grok history
fix; the button and glass changes are in the web bundle it ships.
