# Dispatch v2.47.5 — the Pretty hint's buttons are clickable again

In a CLI thread on desktop, the "Switch to Pretty" and dismiss buttons of the hint at the top
are no longer hidden under the bell and the CLI/Pretty switch. (PR #75.)

## The change

The "Long responses read best in Pretty" hint now keeps space free on its right for the
floating bell and CLI/Pretty switch, so both of its buttons show and can be clicked. In a
narrow center pane, the hint text wraps to two lines. Phones are not affected.

## What was wrong

The bell and the CLI/Pretty switch float over the top-right corner of a thread. In a CLI
thread that corner is the right end of the hint, so the floating controls covered its
"Switch to Pretty" and dismiss buttons, and a click there hit the floating controls.

## Updating

Web app only. Run `dispatch update`, then reload the page.
