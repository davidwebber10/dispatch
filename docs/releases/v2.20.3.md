# Dispatch v2.20.3 — Grok on a phone scrolls while idle, and the screen is flush

## The change

Grok on mobile is more usable:

- A swipe scrolls even while the prompt is idle. Use **PgUp** / **PgDn** on
  the key row as a backup.
- The Grok screen is flush — no card margin, no rounded frame, no extra inset.
- The **Connected** pill no longer sits on the Grok header.
- Coming back to the app refits the terminal so the TUI is not a tiny card
  in empty space.

## What was wrong

Grok ignores mouse-wheel while its prompt is focused. 2.20.2 sent wheel
ticks, so scroll only worked after you sent a message. The card chrome stole
columns. Returning from the background skipped a fit when the box size had
not changed, so Grok kept a stale drawing.

## Under the hood

- Swipes send Page Up/Down (works in both Grok focus modes).
- A Grok thread always uses that PTY path, even off the alternate buffer.
- Visibility and `pageshow` force a fit and always notify the PTY. Fit is
  skipped while the page is hidden.

## Updating

**Web-only** — a browser refresh is enough after `dispatch update`. The
daemon does not need a restart for this change.
