# Dispatch v2.20.0 — Settings is a page, and the panel toggles go home

## The change

**Settings left the modal.** On desktop it is now a full page, a destination like
Threads, Board and Analytics: the rail's gear is a proper nav item with an active
bar, and `⌘,` opens it from anywhere. The page has a vertical section nav —
General, Integrations, Secrets, Tools, Transcription — with a daemon block
underneath (connection, server, version, `~/.dispatch`) and an "autosaves as you
edit" footer. The section content itself is unchanged; there is just finally room
for it. Mobile keeps its drill-down settings exactly as they were.

**The panel toggles moved back up.** The projects and details collapse toggles
sat at the bottom of the icon rail after v2.19.0; they are now back in their old
top-left and top-right corner positions, flanking the tab strip. They stay visible
when their panel is collapsed — a toggle that disappears with its panel could
never bring it back.

## Under the hood

- `SettingsView` renders the same shared section components as mobile; the
  desktop-only `SettingsModal` is deleted.
- The view union gains `'settings'`, so the page persists across reloads like
  the other views.

## Updating

**Web-only** — a browser refresh is enough.
