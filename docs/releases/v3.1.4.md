# Dispatch v3.1.4: photo attach notices and folded thread reports

## The change

- **Photos that can't attach now say so.** Before, if a photo picked on a phone could not be
  used, the picker closed and nothing happened. Now the Chat box shows a notice naming up to
  three skipped files (plus "+N more") and the reason. Any usable photos in the same pick
  still attach. The notice sits on its own line, clears after a good attach or a send, and no
  longer covers a Send-failed or lost-images message.
- **JPEG first in the photo picker.** The picker now asks for JPEG first, so phones that
  convert HEIC photos are more likely to send JPEG than a large PNG.
- **Overlapping photo reads no longer overwrite each other.** If two picks overlap, a slow
  earlier read can't replace the notice from a later one.
- **Thread reports fold in Chat.** A long report from a thread now shows as its bold name plus
  a two-line preview. Tap it to open the full text, which includes an "Open thread" button,
  and tap again to fold it. Short reports (up to 240 characters and 3 lines) stay open. Your
  messages and Dispatch's replies never fold. A long report opened at the bottom of Chat
  stays scrolled to its start. Reports split into pieces label the later ones "(continued)".

Known limits:
- If a photo read stalls, Send stays greyed with no message. Close and reopen the app.
- Reports fold again after a page reload.
- A tall opened report can leave blank space below it until the next send.
- Not yet tested on an iPhone or in Safari.

## Updating

Web app. Run `dispatch update`, then fully close and reopen the app (or reload the page).
