# Dispatch v2.38.1 — mobile model picker stays open

## The change

The Model dropdown in New Thread now stays open on phones. Tap to browse models without
opening the keyboard, or tap the search field to filter the list and choose a model.

## What was wrong

The picker focused its search field immediately and dismissed itself on page scroll or
resize. Mobile keyboard and viewport changes could therefore make it flash open and close.
It now follows the visible viewport, keeps scrolling inside the model list, and still
closes when you choose a model, tap outside, or press Escape.

## Updating

Install with `dispatch update`, then refresh the page on your phone. The fix is in the web
client; updating rebuilds the assets served by your Dispatch host. No database changes.
