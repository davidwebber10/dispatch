# Dispatch v4.10.0: dictation drops um and uh

## The change

- **Dictation now removes filler words.** After speech is turned into text, Dispatch drops "um",
  "uh", "er", "ah" and "hmm" and tidies the commas, spacing and capitals around them. "I, um, think
  so" becomes "I think so." Words that only contain a filler, like "umbrella", are kept, and lists
  keep their commas.
- **New switch: Settings > Dictation > Remove filler words.** It is on by default. Turn it off to get
  the raw text from your speech provider.
- **English only.** Cleanup runs only when the transcript is English, so German "um" and "er" are left
  alone.
- **Why not AssemblyAI's own setting:** Dispatch now asks AssemblyAI to leave fillers out, but its
  Universal-3 Pro model ignores that request, so the word-list pass does the work.

## Known limits

- "like" and "you know" are not removed.
- "I, um, I mean" becomes "I I mean" (repeated words are not merged).
- A few edge cases keep or lose a comma, such as "Part I, um, Part II" and "Red, green, and, um, blue."

## Updating

Run `dispatch update`, then reload the app.
