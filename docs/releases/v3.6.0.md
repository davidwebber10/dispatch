# Dispatch v3.6.0: close status cards, hide the Pretty hint

## The change

- **Close a Done, Blocked or Needs-you card.** Every status card in a thread now has an ×
  in its top-right corner, on mobile and on desktop. Tap it to hide the card. The card stays
  hidden after a refresh. This works in the thread chat, under a CLI thread's terminal, and
  in Chat's own stream.
- **Hide the "Long responses read best in Pretty" bar.** Settings → TERMINAL has a new
  "Pretty hint" switch. Turn it off to hide the bar above every CLI thread. The × on the bar
  turns the same switch off, so you can bring the bar back from Settings.

## Under the hood

- Closed cards are keyed by their content (state + text), not by a transcript id. The same
  card reaches the browser with different ids on the live and the paged paths, and with
  different JSON formatting, so a content key is what makes a dismissal survive a refresh.
- Dismissals live in the browser (localStorage), per thread, capped at 500. They are not
  synced between devices.
- The older per-device "don't show this again" flag for the Pretty hint seeds the new
  setting, so an earlier dismissal still holds.
- New tests: the × on the card, per-thread scoping, content keys, the CLI footer strip, and
  the Pretty hint setting.

## Updating

Web-only. Run `dispatch update`, then reload the app.
