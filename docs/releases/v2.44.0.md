# Dispatch v2.44.0 — overseer recaps and decision cards

The overseer now reports one structured recap per batch of agent work, and every decision
reaches you as a complete card with your own words checked by the daemon. (PRs #62, #63.)

## The change

- **One recap per settled batch.** Every agent notice ends with a Batch line from the daemon:
  which agents still work, or that the batch has settled. While agents work, the overseer
  writes at most one line. When nothing works, it posts one recap. If agents still work 20
  minutes after a finished turn, it posts an interim recap. Type "recap" any time for one now.
- **The recap has fixed sections:** Needs you now, Running on defaults, Your tests and
  actions, Running, Decided since the last recap, Not yet triaged, and Parked.
- **A decision ledger.** Every decision gets its own ID (`N1`, `N2`, …) that is never reused.
  A claim such as "you approved" carries your exact words, and the daemon checks them against
  the messages you really sent.
  - A message that starts with "ok" never counts as an answer.
  - A merge, deploy or release item needs its ID or the action word in your answer, for
    example `N12: merge`. A bare "yes" is not enough.
- **Decision cards.** A decision shows its context, the options with their effects, a
  recommendation with its reason, what happens if you do not answer, and its source (a plan
  or document path, an agent, or a PR). New decisions and the top 5 appear as full cards;
  every other open decision gets one line with its question. Type `show N7` or `show all`.
- **Decisions from agents arrive in the agent's own words.** Planner, researcher and reviewer
  agents end their report with a decisions block. The overseer sends each decision to you
  word for word, or makes a low-level call itself and lists it in the recap. Reply
  `reverse N21` to change such a call.
- **Project rules in your own words.** Tell the overseer a rule, such as "test approach
  decisions come to me", and it records it with your checked words.
- **The Blocked notice** now shows as a notice in the Control Plane stream, not as a raw
  message from you.

## What was wrong

- **Every finished agent turn produced an overseer reply,** so a user who works in several
  projects could not keep up with the stream.
- **Proposals turned into "your rule".** A short "yes" to the overseer's own question was
  later restated as the user's rule, often with a wider scope.
- **Decisions shrank to plan IDs.** Later replies referred to them as "LR-1 to LR-26" or
  "D1 to D9", with no context, and a compaction could drop the list.

## Under the hood

- Two new tables: a log of every message that reaches an overseer, with its sender, and the
  ledger (migrations 005 and 006).
- The dispatch MCP has 26 tools (was 17). The ledger routes accept only the project's
  overseer.
- Agent decision blocks are read from the full final text of the agent's turn, for Claude,
  Codex, Grok and OpenCode agents.
- Each PR passed two review rounds (Fable and GPT-6-astra) and adds tests for every rule.

## Updating

Daemon. Run `dispatch update`. The update restarts every overseer, and each one continues
its own conversation with the new instructions; you do not need a new session. After the
update, ask each overseer once to import its open decisions with `ledger_import`. Each
decision needs a full card. A browser refresh shows the new notice styles.
